Alligator Signal Assistant Config

The configuration settings are available directly in the Extension Popup dropdown.

To configure your settings, simply click the extension icon in your browser toolbar.

Active Configuration Parameters:

  • Trade Stake ($): Used for calculating the manual trade log profits.
  • Convergence Threshold (%): The maximum percentage difference between the three Alligator lines to classify them as "converged" (tangling/consolidation).
  • Loss Cooldown Limit: Number of consecutive manual losses logged before locking the assistant feed.
  • Cooldown Duration (Min): Duration of the signal lock.
